Nether Expansion · design draft 4 · the people

Five peoples of the Nether

The Nether stops being empty. Five peoples live there, each in its own biome, and none of them at peace with the others. The Piglins you already know keep their vanilla look but get new code and their own villages. The Withren, the Wither's own people, keep watch over the soul valleys. The Magmari wade the lava seas, the four-armed Weft weave in the Warped Forest canopy, and the Ghastkin, kin to the ghasts, hang their homes under the cliffs of the Basalt Deltas. The new peoples are box models built with the same kit as the End Expansion people, so what you see is how they would look in game.

The existing mob, rewritten

Piglins

Piglins keep the look they have in Minecraft, Brutes and babies included. What changes is their code. Every Piglin, in a Bastion or in one of their new Holds, will talk to you while you wear gold. Without gold, they try to kill you.

Piglin

PIG-lin · the gold clans of the Holds
Look
Exactly the Minecraft Piglin: same model, same skin, same Brutes and babies, about 1.95 blocks. The mod rewrites only how they behave.
Gold
A piglin's rank, savings and handshake in one. Anyone who shows no gold is an enemy.
Where
In Bastions, as now, and in Holds, their own new villages. Piglins in both can be talked to.
Manner
Loud, boastful and greedy, quick to laugh and quick to swing. Honest in their way: they always tell you exactly what they want, which is your gold.
Names
Short and grunted, with a clan: Grukka Red-Tusk, Snorr Gildsnout, Brakka Ashbristle.
Gender
Male and female, like Village Friends. It shows in names and voices; the vanilla look is the same for both.
Belief
The Overworld rots you. Piglins who walk through a portal turn into zombified piglins, "the Rotted". They can't decide whether you're very brave or already half rotted.

"Gold on your feet. Good. Means I talk first and swing later."

No gold on you

Attacked on sight

  1. Any piglin that sees you charges, in a Bastion or a Hold. Vanilla already starts this; now every piglin nearby joins in.
  2. In a Hold, a Brute blows the war-horn and the whole village comes running.
  3. No talking and no bartering. Right-click does nothing.
Wearing gold

Welcome to talk

  1. Right-click opens the conversation screen.
  2. Talk, barter and earn friendship, counted in gold nuggets. Throwing an ingot still barters, and Trade is in the dock.
  3. They still hate thieves. Open a Hold's chests, mine its gold or touch the Hoard and they turn on you, and remember it.
  4. Take your gold off in front of them and they attack. Hiding your gold is the worst insult a piglin knows.

What counts as gold

  • Any piece of golden armour. This is vanilla's own list (the item tag piglin_safe_armor).
  • Any armour with a gold trim (dashed slot), so you can keep your netherite and still be welcome.

What else changes

  • Holds: their own villages, with homes, jobs and daily routines. Bastions stay theirs too, and the Piglins in them can be talked to just the same.
  • Memory: they know your name, your friendship and your grudges.
  • Brutes respect gold like everyone else (vanilla Brutes attack everyone), except in a Bastion's treasure vault, where only sworn guards may stand.
  • Babies follow gold-wearers around and try to borrow a nugget.
  • Enemies: Piglins attack Withren and Magmari on sight. A Magmari can never show gold, because it melts on their skin.
  • Zombified piglins stay as they are: the Rotted.

How: in 26.3 the gold check is PiglinAi.isWearingSafeArmor (item tag minecraft:piglin_safe_armor) and right-click is PiglinAi.mobInteract. The rewrite hooks those two and the attack-target search. Their models and textures are untouched, so Piglins stay vanilla mobs and keep working with resource packs and other mods.

The Wither's own people

Withren

A player's body and height, with the Wither's head at normal head size. The Withren are neutral: they leave you alone unless you hurt one of them. They made the Wither once, as a last resort, and whatever they say about it comes back to the price: three of their own.

Withren

WITH-ren · keepers of the soul valleys
Body
The player's shape and height, 1.8 blocks. Ash-dark skin and bony knuckles.
Head
The Wither's own head at normal head size. In game it is drawn from the Wither's texture, so it matches the Wither exactly and changes with resource packs.
Gender
None. Every Withren is "they".
Temper
Neutral. They ignore you and go about their day. Hit one and every Withren nearby fights back, and the Vigil remembers.
Dress
Soul-cloth in slate and soul-soil brown, bone toggles, soul lanterns. Elders wear hoods and a stole with a tally mark for every name they keep. Guards wear basalt carved with the Wither's ribcage.
Manner
Quiet, grave and formal, with a dry graveyard humour. They keep promises and they keep names.
Names
Each Withren carries the name of one dead person they have sworn to remember: Sereth, who keeps Anwen. Corvane, who keeps the Three.
Children
The classic Minecraft baby: the adult's body and clothes at half size, but the same full-size Wither head, so a child is mostly skull. About 1.25 blocks. A child has no one to keep yet; they choose a name at their first Vigil.
Wart-growerBrewerBonecarverLantern-keeperRemembrancer (elder)Ashwarden (guard)

"The Wither is not a weapon. It is the end of an argument. We have only ever ended one."

The Last Answer

A weapon of mass destruction, made only as a final resort. It takes three sacrifices.

  • Three Withren give themselves willingly. Their skulls become its three heads, and the souls in the soul sand become its body.
  • It can't be aimed and it can't be called back. It burns whatever is in front of it until the three souls inside are spent.
  • It was made once, at the end of the War of the Hoard. It broke the Piglin bastions into the ruins you find today, then turned on the Withren's own fortresses.
  • Wither skeletons are the Hollowed: Withren whose souls it burned out as it passed. Their bodies still walk the old fortresses.
  • Three Hollowed skulls on soul sand also wake a Wither, but one with no willing souls inside, so nothing in it wants to stop. That is how outsiders make Withers.

You carry wither skulls

They notice. They ask, carefully, what you mean to do with them.

You summon a Wither

Every Vigil hears of it. Friendship with every Withren drops hard. They stay neutral but go cold.

You kill a Wither

They honour you. The Nether Star is the three souls fused. Lay it to rest in a Hall of the Three for a great reward, or keep it for a beacon.

The third people

Magmari

Nobody has settled the lava seas, so the third people live on them. The Magmari wade the lava, herd striders and run ferries. They never fight you, but they want nothing to do with the Piglins or the Withren.

Magmari

mag-MAR-ee · the lava-sea folk
Body
About 2.5 blocks and hunched, with long backward-bending legs for wading and a short heavy tail.
Skin
Magma crust: dark plates with glowing seams. A crest of basalt columns runs over the head, and each column is a little vent.
Heat
Lava doesn't hurt them; cold does. Away from lava or fire they cool like a strider: the glow fades, the crust turns ash-violet, and they get slow and grumpy. A wade or a seat by a vent warms them up.
Temper
Peaceful toward you. Attack one and it wades out into the lava where you can't follow, and the Mooring remembers. Piglin and Withren raiders get the chain bridges pulled up and magma thrown at them.
Dress
Twisting-vine rope, hip cloths woven from weeping vines, iron chain. They are the Nether's chainwrights.
Manner
Warm, chatty and hospitable, fond of songs and haggling. Always cold, always offering you a seat by the magma.
Names
Volcanic words, with their mooring's chain: Tephra of the Long Chain, Scoria of the Slow Glow, Brann, Lapilli, Tuff.
Gender
Male and female. Males carry one tall crest ridge and a chain sash; females twin ridges hung with chain loops and a woven chest wrap.
Children
About 1.3 blocks: round, stubby-legged, with three nubs for a crest. They run hotter than adults but cool twice as fast, so they never stray far from a vent.
Ferrier (strider barges)Lava-anglerChainwrightQuartzcutterHearthkeeperChartmaker

"Brr. You're cold as a ghast's tear. Sit by the vent. Sit, sit."

Why they matter in play

  • Ferries: hire a strider barge across a lava sea (the Travel button in the dock). They carry outsiders like you, never one of the other four peoples.
  • Striders are their cattle and their boats. Ghasts are "the sky-mourners": the Magmari say they saw the Wither and have been crying ever since.
The fourth people

Weft

The giant fungi of the Warped Forest hold the Weft: four-armed weavers who live in the canopy and spin twisting vines into cloth, nets and bridges. Their saying is that the forest is the warp and they are the weft. They are wary: leave their forest alone and they will talk; cut it and they drop nets on you.

Weft

WEFT · the canopy weavers
Body
About 2.3 blocks and slender, with four arms: a long upper pair to climb and a short lower pair to weave. Long three-fingered hands.
Head
A smooth dark face plate with six small glowing eyes, a long skull swept back, and two dark mandibles at the jaw.
Skin
Like warped-stem bark: deep indigo with teal streaks.
Temper
Wary. They talk to visitors who leave the forest alone. Break warped stems or wart near a Loom and they drop vine nets on you, slowing and holding you, and the whole Loom turns on you.
Dress
Cloth woven from twisting vines: warped-wart teal with shroomlight-orange stitching, spools at the belt, a shuttle always in a lower hand.
Gender
Male and female. Males wear a shoulder mantle, a short kilt and a woven headband; females a shawl, a long skirt and a headwrap with two long tails.
Manner
Patient, precise and quietly smug about having four arms. They click their mandibles while they think.
Names
A name and the row of the Long Cloth woven the year they were born: Vesk, Row Four-Hundred-Nine.
Children
About 1.1 blocks, with only two arms. The lower pair are nubs that grow in later.
WeaverSpinnerNetcaster (guard)DyerCanopy-tenderClothkeeper (elder)

"The forest is the warp. We are the weft. You are a loose thread, ground-walker."

The Long Cloth

  • Every Loom keeps a copy of the Long Cloth: their whole history, woven one row a year since the first fungus grew.
  • Sixty rows in the middle are black. That is the Wither, and they don't talk about it.
  • Do something worth remembering for a Loom and they weave you into the cloth: their highest honour, and a friendship milestone.
The fifth people

Ghastkin

Under the cliffs of the Basalt Deltas hang the Eaves of the Ghastkin: pale, floating mourners with faces like ghasts. They are the gentlest people in the Nether and the most easily frightened. Come at them fast and they drift up out of reach; come softly, sneaking, and they will talk.

Ghastkin

GAST-kin · the mourners of the Basalt Deltas
Body
A pale, slender upper body, and below the waist six tentacles instead of legs. They float a little off the ground, about 2.25 blocks from crown to tentacle tip.
Head
A ghast's face at normal head size: closed eyes, tear tracks, a small sad mouth.
Temper
Shy and peaceful. Approach while sneaking to talk. Run at them or draw a weapon and they drift up toward the ceiling. They never fight.
Tears
They cry once, properly, for each death, and then let it go. Their tears close wounds and wash away memories, so they trade healing and are careful who they cry for.
Dress
Ash-silk the colour of delta smoke, and a tear vial on a cord.
Gender
Male and female. Males wear a hood and a short cape; females a long sheer veil and a circlet of tear beads.
Manner
Soft-spoken, kind and a little melancholy, with a sly streak. They speak slowly and never finish a sad story.
Names
They take a new name after every great grief and let the old one go: Siel, who was Oriel.
Children
About 1.2 blocks, eyes still open: they haven't learned to cry yet.
Ghasts
A Ghastkin who grieves too long swells, forgets their hands and their words, and drifts off as a ghast, still crying.
Tearkeeper (healer)MenderSongwardenAshsweeperDrift-watcher

"We cry for them once, properly. Then we let them go. It's the letting go that takes practice."

Their beasts

Three creatures

The Piglins have their hoglins and the Magmari their striders. The other three peoples each keep a beast of their own, which lives wild in their biome and in their villages.

Cairnback

the Withren's beast · Soul Sand Valley
Cairnback
The Withren's slow tortoise. Its high, stepped shell is plated in bone like a cairn, and a small soul fire burns in a bowl on top for as long as it lives. Hold a torch, lantern or campfire to it and it comes away relit with soul fire. Bred with nether wart. Every Hall of the Three keeps one; more graze the wart terraces.
Silkgrub
The Weft's fat teal caterpillar, with glowing orange spots. Shear its tufts for string; they grow back in three minutes, or at once if you feed it warped roots. It climbs like the Weft. Bred with warped roots. Kept on the Loom's decks.
Ashbell
The Ghastkin's pale drifter, like a jellyfish of the air, half see-through with a glow at its heart. It rings faintly as it goes and can be led on a lead. No ghast takes aim at anyone within 16 blocks of one: a ghast that hears an Ashbell remembers it was kin. It drifts round the Eave's pillars and over the pool of tears.
Young
Half size. A young Cairnback has no fire yet and a young Silkgrub no silk.

"A faint ringing in the smoke is an Ashbell. A scream is a ghast. It helps to learn the difference quickly."

How they get along

Five peoples, no peace

They don't share villages and they don't share the Nether. Every pair has its own grudge, and they fight wherever they meet. On top of that, Piglins attack anyone who shows no gold, and none of the other four ever wears it.

In the game

Screenshots from the mod's own test: it makes a new world, finds each kind of village by natural generation in the Nether, checks the residents are there, and photographs it.

The vanilla ruins stay and get a past. Bastion Remnants are what the Wither left of the Piglin empire, and Brutes still guard the old hoards. Nether Fortresses are the Withren's old war-forts, now held by the Hollowed and by the blazes they once set there as wardens.

History

The Hoard, the War and the Wither

  1. The Gilded Age

    Five peoples, five realms, no friendship. Piglin bastions roofed in gold across the wastes, Withren valleys and nether-brick fortresses, Magmari on the lava seas, Weft looms in the warped canopy, Ghastkin Eaves under the delta cliffs.

  2. The War of the Hoard

    The Piglins want the gold under the valleys. The Magmari ferry Piglin war-bands across the lava for pay, and the Weft strip bone from both sides' dead. Piglins wear gold to tell friend from foe, because nobody else will.

  3. The Last Answer

    Three Withren give themselves and the Wither wakes. It burns the bastions, the Moorings and the Withren's own fortresses. The grief afterwards is so great that half the Ghastkin swell into ghasts, and the Weft weave sixty black rows.

  4. You arrive

    Five peoples, five kinds of village, ten grudges. Then you step through a portal, from the world where outsiders make Withers for fun.

Decisions so far

  • Piglins keep their vanilla model and skin; only their code changes. Every Piglin can be talked to while you wear gold, in Bastions as well as Holds.
  • Gold means golden armour or any armour with a gold trim. Brutes respect it, except in a Bastion's treasure vault.
  • Withren wear the Wither's own head, drawn from the game's Wither texture, and are genderless. Summoning a Wither turns them cold, not hostile.
  • Magmari are peaceful toward you.
  • Gender: male and female like Village Friends for Piglins, Magmari, Weft and Ghastkin. Withren have none.
  • Babies for everyone: the vanilla baby Piglin, Withren children in the classic style, young Magmari, Weft and Ghastkin.
  • Names: the Weft keep theirs. The Basalt Deltas people are the Ghastkin, named for the ghasts they become. Both tempers stand: the Weft wary, the Ghastkin shy.
  • Villages are never shared: Holds, Vigils, Moorings, Looms and Eaves, and the five peoples fight wherever they meet.

Next: the five villages are being built in the game itself, then the few creature species, the dialogue bank and the screen. Animations stay on hold.
